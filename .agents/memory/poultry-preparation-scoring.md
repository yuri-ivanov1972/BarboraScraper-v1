---
name: Minced poultry preparation scoring
description: Why verified merchandising categories must not transfer raw-meat processing scores to preparations.
---

A verified minced-meat merchandising category can contain both raw meat and
salted/flavoured preparations. Verify the actual preparation form and treat fiber
defaults, Nutri-Score food type and NOVA evidence independently. A valid Rimi
category mapping does not make its inherited NOVA 1 correct for a preparation.

**Why:** Source checks found an identical flavoured chicken preparation and a
comparable salted/flavoured turkey preparation in Rimi's mixed minced-meat
category. The category inherited NOVA 1 despite preparation-specific markers.
The user explicitly required no raw-meat assumptions or invented mapping.

**How to apply:** Check accepted ingredient evidence before inheriting NOVA.
Flavourings identify processing independently of a health-concern severity.
Do not import the reference category's red-meat Nutri-Score type for poultry.
Keep any configured zero fiber visibly estimated, not measured or inferred from
an absent label field. Separate precautionary allergen warnings from ingredients
without removing their original label text.
